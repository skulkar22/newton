Add `rigid_consistency="project"` to `ModalGeneratorCraigBamptonFEM`: it removes a small rigid-body residual of the stiffness matrix (for example from warped shells of a non-conformal mesh) from the reduced model with minimum-norm corrections of the stiffness, damping, mass and recoveries, reports the residuals before and after, and refuses residuals or eigenvalue changes beyond `rigid_projection_limit` and `rigid_projection_eigenvalue_tolerance`. The recovery tolerance of `ModalGeneratorCraigBampton.build()` is now the parameter `rigid_recovery_tolerance` (default 1e-6, unchanged).
