Warn at `CollisionPipeline` construction when a mesh that can collide with a reduced elastic body is open, non-manifold, or inside-out, and add the experimental `elastic_contact_mesh_sign_method` option. Its `"normal_and_parity"` value counts a contact sample as inside a mesh only where the pseudo-normal and a three-ray parity test both say inside: this removes most wrong-side contacts next to open cups or duplicated faces, but not behind single open sheets, and it drops a share of real contacts, all of them on an inside-out mesh. Closed, outward-facing collision meshes with small contact gaps on both shapes remain the recommended fix; the default `"normal"` is unchanged.
