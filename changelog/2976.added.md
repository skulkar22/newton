Add experimental finite-friction sigmoid material projection for XPBD and VBD through `tendon_material_direct=True`, with a mixed-precision cooperative CUDA implementation, a scalar CPU implementation, and explicit numerical-failure reporting; retain material sweeps as the default.
Allow slip to repair temporary over-bound rolling trials and preserve valid slack spans shorter than the minimum rest length, while enforcing that minimum on the final material allocation.
Add experimental massless routed tendons with anchors, pinholes, circular rollers,
finite capstan friction, and isolated dynamic routing in XPBD and VBD. Complete
routes are authored with `ModelBuilder.add_tendon()` and `TendonGuide` entries,
including world-attached guides with `body=-1`, and are preserved by builder
merging, world replication, and fixed-joint collapse.
Native tendon, guide, and segment attribute domains support labels and custom
attributes. Material and routing history is stored in `State` for copying and
replay, with explicit runtime-property update and reset semantics.
