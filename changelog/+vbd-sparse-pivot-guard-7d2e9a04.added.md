Guard the 6 x 6 block Cholesky pivots of the `SolverVBD` block-sparse articulation solves: a pivot that is not positive enough is counted and clamped to a documented relative floor, and the solver exposes the count for the current step as `rigid_articulation_pivot_clamped` (device int32); a non-zero count means the float32 factorization of that step was not trustworthy. Unclamped factorizations are bitwise unchanged.
