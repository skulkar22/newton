Support reduced elastic links in `SolverVBD(rigid_articulation_solve="block_sparse_joints")`:

  - Each articulation that contains an elastic link is factored as one system with a `(6 + m)` block per elastic link, so directly jointed elastic links are coupled exactly and joints act at the deformed attachments. Rigid-only articulations keep the 6 x 6 solve. An articulation whose only body is an elastic link, such as one created by `ModelBuilder.add_body_elastic()`, is now solved by the local block solve with `elastic_body_relaxation` and may be jointed to bodies outside articulation ranges.
  - The experimental `rigid_articulation_elastic_solve` option selects this `"coupled"` solve (default) or `"schur"`, which eliminates each link's modes onto the frame blocks of the link and its joint neighbors and is kept for comparison.
  - A failed factorization takes a diagonal step, is recorded by `SolverVBD.rigid_articulation_elastic_solve_status()`, counted by `SolverVBD.rigid_articulation_elastic_solve_failure_count()` and reported by one warning per solver, and can be addressed with `rigid_articulation_diagonal_regularization`; `SolverVBD.elastic_mode_solve_metrics()` reports NaN for bodies whose metrics were not evaluated.
