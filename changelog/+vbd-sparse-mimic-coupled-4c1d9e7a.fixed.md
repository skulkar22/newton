Fix `SolverVBD` joint mimics under `rigid_articulation_solve="block_sparse_joints"` and `"block_sparse_levels"` when the mimic's bodies are connected by joints to other bodies of the articulation (serial revolute chains, gripper fingers on a moving base): the multiplier step used the bodies' own Hessian blocks, underestimated the compliance of the coupled articulation solve several times over, and the iteration diverged, separating the joints by 0.6 m to tens of meters. Mimic rows on block-sparse articulation bodies now use the articulation's factored system for the compliance and the pose update. Models without mimics and the `"local"` solve are unchanged.
